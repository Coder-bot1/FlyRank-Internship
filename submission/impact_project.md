## 1. Protocol: How to Add the Next Case Study
To ensure the portfolio remains an active career platform rather than a static course artifact, every future project follows this 4-step repeatable protocol:

```text
[Step 1: Data Contract] ──► [Step 2: 3-Beat Interview] ──► [Step 3: Edit & Verify] ──► [Step 4: 10-Min Deploy]
```

### The 3-Beat Standard Shape:
1. **Beat 1: The Problem (The Tension)**
   - Name the specific operational bottleneck (e.g., editorial bandwidth, traffic loss).
   - Highlight the flaw in existing naive heuristics or standard industry practices.
2. **Beat 2: What I Did (and What I Decided)**
   - Detail the architecture: data inputs, model choice, and features.
   - State explicit decisions: what was deliberately excluded, how leakage was prevented, and how validation was guarded (e.g. grouped splits).
3. **Beat 3: What Came of It (The Proof)**
   - Report verifiable numbers: precision lift, error reduction, or speedup.
   - Link directly to the live artifact: deployed research page, code repository, and reason-coded output queues.
